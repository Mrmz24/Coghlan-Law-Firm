COMMON QUESTIONS

Answers to help you take the next step.

General answers to help you prepare for an enquiry. Your specific circumstances may require tailored advice.

What should I include in my first enquiry?

Briefly describe the issue, relevant dates, the people or organisations involved and any deadlines you know about. Avoid sending highly sensitive documents until a suitable method is agreed.

What documents should I bring to a consultation?

Bring relevant contracts, letters, notices, records and a short timeline of events, if available. The documents needed will depend on your matter.

Can I receive advice before deciding what to do?

A consultation can help you explain the issue, understand potential options and identify questions that need further consideration.

How do I arrange a consultation?

Use the consultation or contact page to send an enquiry. Include your preferred contact method and a suitable time to respond.

Does information on this website count as legal advice?

No. Website information is general in nature and does not create a lawyer-client relationship.
